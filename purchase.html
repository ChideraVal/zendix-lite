How Pages Work

Pages are deducted based on the amount of content processed, making it easy to estimate how many pages you need before purchasing.

📄

PDF Documents

Every page in a PDF counts as one page. A 12-page PDF uses 12 pages from your balance.

Example: 12-page PDF → 12 Pages
🖼

Images

Every uploaded image counts as one page, regardless of its dimensions.

Example: 5 Images → 5 Pages
📦

Multiple Files

Pages are combined across every file included in the same extraction request.

Example: 8-page PDF + 3 Images → 11 Pages
